date: 2026-10-16
summary: A device that loses its key says so, and the app holds on to its connection.
---

- **"Key lost" instead of "Not paired"**: when the browser deletes the data of the app (usually
  because the device is low on space), the key of the device goes with it. The app now tells you,
  on the Dashboard and in the project, and says what to do: remove the computer from this device
  and pair again. Your projects stay on the computer.
- **A notice about the storage of the app**: after the browser deleted its data, and once when the
  browser has not promised to keep it, with how to make that less likely. Dismiss it and it comes
  back only after a new deletion.
- **A device key is used only once it is saved**: if the browser can't save it, the app asks you to
  open it again instead of pairing with a key that would be gone at the next start.
- **Fewer disconnections**: a connection broken on a bad network is tried again, instead of showing
  "Not paired".
- **Notifications turn themselves back on** when the browser dropped them, as long as they are
  still allowed for the site.
- **New version available**: a yellow dot on the ⋯ of the Dashboard when the app has a new
  version, and a tap loads it, also in the app on the Home Screen, which doesn't reload by itself.
- The version of the app at the foot of the Dashboard.
- The log of palmtop on the computer (`~/.palmtopai/daemon.log`) names the device it refuses.
- Update with `palmtop update`. Existing pairings keep working.
