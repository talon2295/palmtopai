summary: Notifications also reach a locked phone.
---

- **Notifications with the screen locked**: they are sent as urgent, so Android and iOS deliver
  them right away also when the phone is idle, and the push service keeps them for up to 4 days
  while the device is off.
- A phone locked with the app open no longer counts as looking at the project: after 30 seconds
  without news from it, the computer notifies it again.
- **A new app**: a Dashboard with one card per computer, its projects and what is left to review,
  and a "Working now" strip that opens the project where the agent is working. Inside a computer,
  `project › session` opens a sheet to move between them. Built on shadcn/ui, with the same look.
