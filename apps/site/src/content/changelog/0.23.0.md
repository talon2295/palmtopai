date: 2026-10-17
summary: Links you can open, a note made of elements, and screenshots on notes.
---

- **Links**: an address in a message from the agent, in a check, in a note or in what somebody
  wrote to you opens in a new tab: `https://example.com/page` or just `example.com`. Names of
  code files such as `README.md` or `main.py` are not taken for addresses.
- **The note for the agent is a list**: add, edit and delete its elements one by one instead of
  rewriting one text. The agent still reads one note, and now sees which elements are new
  (`[new]`) and which you edited (`[edit]`) since it last read it.
- **Screenshots**: attach one to the note of a check or to an element of the note for the agent,
  from the gallery, the camera or by pasting. It is shrunk on your device (the location of a photo
  is dropped), sent to your computer and shown as a picture under the note: tap it to open it.
  Your agent gets the file, and with the palmtop tools the picture itself.
- **Offline**: a screenshot taken while the computer is off waits on your device and is sent,
  before the note that names it, once the computer is back.
- **Shared checklists**: the people you share with can attach screenshots too, within a smaller
  space. You see them under their answer, and your agent receives them with what they wrote.
- Your agent's message can now be a few lines with numbered steps, for what you have to do and in
  which order.
- Update with `palmtop update` on every computer: screenshots need palmtop 0.23 on the computer.
  An older one still works, but your agent then reads the note with the names of its elements and
  without marks. Existing pairings keep working.
