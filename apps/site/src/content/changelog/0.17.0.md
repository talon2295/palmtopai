- **New address**: the site, the app and the relay live at
  [palmtop.mtwa.it](https://palmtop.mtwa.it). Installing and updating use it from now on.
- The address used before keeps answering the same way, so nothing breaks: computers installed
  earlier go on working until you run `palmtop update`, and a device paired there stays paired.
- Your devices belong to the address they were paired on: to move one to the new one, open
  [app.palmtop.mtwa.it](https://app.palmtop.mtwa.it/) and pair it again from there.
- Fixed in 0.17.1: a question counted as many things to review as it had options. It counts as
  one, and stops counting as soon as you answer it — on the Dashboard, in the session and in what
  the agent reads.
