date: 2026-09-30
summary: "Questions from your agent: it sends the options, you choose on your devices."
---

- **Questions**: when the agent is stuck between real alternatives it sends them to your devices
  instead of a checklist. The options come one card at a time, each with a couple of lines on what
  it means: answer **Pick**, **No** or **Ask**, with a note if useful. See
  [Answering a question](/docs/app#answering-a-question).
- **Your own answer**: the last card is yours. Write what you would do instead and the agent takes
  it as the decision.
- Once answered, a question closes into a row with the option you chose. Tap the row to open it
  again and change your answer.
- Notifications tell a question from a checklist, with its title only, as always.
- Fixed in 0.16.1: a question written while a device still had the previous version of the app
  was shown there as a plain checklist, and stayed that way. Devices now ask their computer for a
  fresh copy after an update that adds fields.
- For agents: `palmtop choice create`, with an `--option` (and an optional `--detail`) per
  alternative, plus `--multiple` when more than one can be chosen. See
  [CLI](/docs/cli#checklists-need---session).
