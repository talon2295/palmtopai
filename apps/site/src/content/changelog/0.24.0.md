date: 2026-10-20
summary: Tell your agent you finished reviewing with one tap, and it picks your review up by itself.
---

- **Review done**: a button at the bottom of a session tells your agent you finished reviewing. It
  asks first, says how many checks are still not reviewed, and turns into "Marked as done" until your
  agent writes something new in the session.
- **No message in the chat**: Claude Code waits for your tap with the new `palmtop wait` and reads
  your review by itself. It waits only when there is something to wait for. This works only with
  Claude Code for now: other agents (Codex included) are not supported, tell them in the chat, as
  before.
- Reading your review spends the tap: the button comes back, so you can tap again if you have more to
  check. There is one wait for each session, a wait nobody taps for ends after a day, and
  `palmtop status` shows which sessions are waited in.
- The button is lit only while an agent is waiting for it, so a tap never goes nowhere; when none
  waits it says to tell the agent in the chat.
- **Shared checklists**: a person can tap **Review done** on their copy. You get a notification and
  the app shows it next to their name; your agent is not told, it reads their answers as always.
- `palmtop sessions` and `palmtop session list` mark a session you tapped as `[done]`.
- Update with `palmtop update` on every computer: **Review done** needs palmtop 0.24 on the computer.
  Existing pairings keep working.
