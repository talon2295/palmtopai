date: 2026-11-05
summary: What your agent writes shows formatted, and a command or a string has a button to copy it.
---

- **A title on every check**: the first line of a check shows in bold, so a simple check stays clear
  on its own. A check that needs steps keeps the same first line, with the rest in normal text
  under it.
- **Formatted text from your agent**: checks, the questions and options of a choice and the message
  at the top of a session can have bold and italic, `code`, lists and links. A link written with a
  label shows the real address next to it.
- **Copy button**: a command or a string your agent wants you to take is in a box on its own, with a
  button that copies it exactly as written and turns into a green check for a moment. Handy when
  you use palmtop on a computer.
- Your own words stay plain: notes, the checks and options you add, and what the people you share
  with write show as typed (addresses still open). Images and HTML are not supported.
- The agent guide and the tools say how to write it. Update with `palmtop update` on every
  computer: an agent on 0.24 or older writes plain text, which shows as before.
