date: 2026-09-28
summary: Pair the app on the Home Screen with a code, and an invitation to install it.
---

- **Pairing with a code**: the app added to the Home Screen shows a code of six letters and digits.
  Run `palmtop pair CODE` on your computer, confirm, then tap **Pair** on the device. On iPhone the
  app on the Home Screen doesn't share Safari's storage, so a pairing made in the browser was not
  there. See [On the Home Screen](/docs/app#on-the-home-screen).
- **Install app**: on phones and tablets the app invites you to add it to the Home Screen, with the
  steps for iPhone and Android. Also in the menu of the Dashboard.
- `palmtop pair` and the pairing page mention the code for the app on the Home Screen.
