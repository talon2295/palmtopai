summary: Archive sessions and projects you are done with.
---

- **Archive**: put a session away from the ⋯ menu of the project, or a whole project from the ⋯
  next to it on the Dashboard. It leaves the Dashboard and its counts, on every device paired with
  that computer, and comes back by itself when your agent writes to it again.
- **The archive** next to the bell lists what you archived, by computer and project: open a
  session as it is, or restore it.
- `palmtop session list` and `palmtop sessions` mark archived sessions `[archived]`.
- Update with `palmtop update`: archiving needs palmtop 0.20 on the computer. Existing pairings
  keep working.
