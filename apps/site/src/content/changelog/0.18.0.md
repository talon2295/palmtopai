---
date: 2026-10-06
summary: End-to-end encryption, and the app on its own address.
---

- **End-to-end encrypted**: everything between your computer and your devices is encrypted, and
  only they can read your checklists. The relay forwards data it can't read and stores nothing.
  See [Security and privacy](/docs/security).
- **A key for each device**, made in the browser and impossible to copy out of it. The token every
  device used to share is gone.
- **`palmtop devices`** lists the devices paired with a computer; `palmtop devices remove` revokes
  one, which is disconnected at once. **Unpair this computer…** in the app revokes the device on
  the computer too.
- **Pairing with a code** shows the same 6-digit number in the terminal and on the device: confirm
  it on both, and nothing pairs if it differs.
- **The app moves to [app.palmtop.mtwa.it](https://app.palmtop.mtwa.it)**, an address of its own.
  Old links to `/app/` take you there.
- **Pair each device again, once**: run `palmtop update` on each computer, then `palmtop pair`.
  Pairings made before 0.18 don't work any more. Your projects and checklists stay on the
  computer. On a phone, add the app to the Home Screen again from the new address, and turn
  notifications on again.
- The app needs Safari 17, Chrome 137, Firefox 130 or newer.
- In 0.18.1, clearer instructions for agents: reviews read as `works`, `fails`, `question` or
  `not reviewed`; the agent offers palmtop when it is about to give you things to test by hand,
  and once you used it in a conversation it keeps putting the checks of later work there; after
  a fix it edits the item, so it comes back to review on your devices; and it never unpairs
  your devices (`palmtop devices remove`) unless you ask.
- In the app, with **Hide reviewed** on, lists with nothing left to review and questions you
  answered move below the others, so what is left to do stays at the top.
