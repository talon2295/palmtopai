summary: Notifications on your devices.
---

- Turn on notifications from the ⋯ menu of a project: your devices are notified when the agent
  creates a checklist, adds checks or sets a message.
- Notifications show only the computer, the project and a title, never the text of your checks.
- No notification while the app is open on screen. On iPhone and iPad, from the app added to the
  Home Screen.
