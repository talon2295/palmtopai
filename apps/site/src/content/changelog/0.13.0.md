---
date: 2026-09-25
summary: Install with one command.
---

- **Installer**: `curl -fsSL https://palmtop.mtwa.it/install.sh | bash` downloads
  palmtop, checks its SHA-256 and sets it up. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started#install).
- **`palmtop setup`**, run by the installer: choose the agents that should use palmtop (Claude
  Code, Codex; only the installed ones, unless `--force-agents-install`), add palmtop to your
  `PATH` and pair a device with the QR code, all from the terminal. Without a terminal it asks
  nothing and prints the steps left.
- `palmtop install` is replaced by `palmtop setup`.
