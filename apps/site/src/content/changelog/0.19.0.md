summary: Share a checklist with a client, a colleague or a friend.
---

- **Share a checklist with other people.** Give someone a copy of a checklist of checks to verify on
  their own device: they see only that, never your computer or your other projects. You see what
  each of them answered under every item, with their notes, and your agent puts the answers
  together. See [Sharing with other people](/docs/sharing).
- **`palmtop invite --name Mario`** shows a link and a QR code that open once, within 24 hours,
  with no account and nothing to install. Only you can run it, in a terminal. You get a
  notification when they join.
- **`palmtop share`, `retest`, `people`**: give a copy, bring it up to date with `retest` when you
  want (an item whose text changed asks for a new review; reviews of unchanged items stay), see who
  has what, take a single checklist out of a copy (`share remove`) or stop sharing
  (`share close`). `palmtop message --to` writes to one person only, and
  `palmtop session owner-name` sets how they see your name.
- **In the app**, a chip with the name of each person under every item, and **Hide results** to
  hide them. What somebody shares with you opens from their link, under their name, with the same
  notes and offline copy; when their date passes it turns read-only.
- **Your agent** learned to share, to compare the answers when people disagree and to bring a copy
  up to date after a fix, and never to invite anyone.
- Update with `palmtop update`. Existing pairings keep working.
